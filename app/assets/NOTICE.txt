Community artwork in this adaptation

The current PNG icons are generated by scripts/generate-community-assets.py
from simple drawing primitives, not extracted from the Nahimic vendor package.
They are covered by the project's MIT license. Legacy filenames are retained
for UI compatibility; no SteelSeries logo is reproduced.

The upstream project previously used original Nahimic artwork owned by
A-Volute / SteelSeries. This adaptation replaces that artwork in its current
source tree and removes the original panel screenshot. Historical upstream
commits retain their original attribution and licensing notices.

Nahimic, MSI, A-Volute and SteelSeries names/trademarks remain owned by their
respective holders. This is an independent community adaptation.
